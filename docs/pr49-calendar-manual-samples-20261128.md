# Calendar-aware scheduling and manual sample identities

PR49 adds deterministic calendar validation beneath E2's verified candidate proposals. Priority order is 急件 → 專案 → 一般 → 委外; existing signed bookings are not preempted and scheduling never supplies an approval or signature.

Trial administrators can choose uninterrupted operation, workday segments, uninterrupted workday windows, continuous compound stages or discontinuous compound stages. Weekend/holiday operation, attended work hours, overtime cutoff and compatible-machine substitution are explicit settings. Physical method, sample capacity and signed trial effort remain authoritative. Discontinuous stages may wait for workdays; equipment and executor reservations conservatively cover the whole chain, including waiting periods.

Government office holidays are pinned for 2026 and 2027. Reference: [data.gov.tw dataset 14718](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/14718), via [TaiwanCalendar](https://github.com/ruyut/TaiwanCalendar). The curated snapshot is attributed to data.gov.tw under TaiwanCalendar's [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) attribution. The SQL migration contains all 730 reference dates. Coverage is shown in the interface; unknown years fail closed. This is an office calendar reference, not a claim that every workplace has identical holiday rules.

Typhoon closures must be entered by an authorized workspace administrator from the actual local announcement; the application does not guess an affected region. Equipment managers can declare a dated equipment fault. Recording an incident immediately checks affected real schedules; the existing authenticated service clock repeats this work every 30 seconds without needing an open browser.

Approved, unstarted schedules move forward or use an available compatible device. Substitution requires matching category and a nonempty, exactly matching specification as well as existing method/capacity checks. Running trials pause with an interruption record and retain their start/signature history. Human confirmation is required to resume a resumable trial or restart its full effort; uninterrupted trials require retest. Resume conservatively uses its original devices. Moving or restarting a schedule clears receipt and requires fresh preparation confirmation. If the bounded search finds no safe plan, an audited BLOCKED entry prevents automatic start and is retried. It never invents availability or extends a trial's exposure to hide a holiday.

Sample numbers are now entered manually in exactly one field per requested sample (1–1000), then explicitly saved as one atomic group. `RT-2` and `#rt-2` normalize to `#RT-2`. Two distinct uppercase letters, a hyphen and a positive integer are required. Repeated letters (`RR-2`), duplicate identities (`RT-2, ST-3, ST-3`), blank fields and count mismatches are rejected with corrections before saving. Native first approval rechecks the complete group regardless of the optional AI switch. Previously saved groups remain intact. Before first approval the whole group may be deleted and re-entered; after approval it is frozen. Historical identities remain reserved for traceability and cannot be reused. Older generation RPCs refuse automatic generation.

Migrations 056 and 057 add private calendar/incident/receipt tables and a manual group RPC. They rewrite no existing case, sample group, role, member or signed document. Existing function grants/owners and unrelated functions are preserved. No report signing key rotation, live case fixtures, simulated workflow advance, model calls, email dispatch, Production frontend deployment or PR merge are part of this delivery.

Validation: full Node suite (1048), focused transport/calendar suite (147), local rollback-only manual groups (32), calendar/incident lifecycle (34), continuous/discontinuous compounds (24), preflight (25), request permissions (40), human-flow regression (38) and trial taxonomy (9), for 202 rollback-only SQL checks. Desktop/mobile manual workflow includes invalid fields and receipt recovery. Calendar controls separately exercise policy/incident save, interrupted-trial decisions and unknown-outcome recovery.
